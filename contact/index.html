Frequently asked questions

Is Nubonta the same as Healthy Bones Co.?

Yes. Nubonta is the new name of Healthy Bones Co. The formulas, the team and the standards stay the same; only the name and the packaging are new.

Where should I start?

Most people begin with Collagen Peptides, one scoop a day. If you want a complete routine, the Bone Renewal Ritual bundle combines collagen, creatine + HMB and electrolytes.

How does subscribe & save work?

Subscriptions save 10% on every order. You choose how often it ships, and you can skip, pause or cancel any time from your account.

How long until I see results?

Consistency matters most. Customers who take their formulas daily for three months or more tend to report the best results.*
