Kevin’s journey

Marine. Patient. Coach. Founder.

  1. The U.S. Marine Corps

    After serving five years in the U.S. Marine Corps, including multiple deployments in the infantry, Kevin Ellis returned stateside only to face a different kind of battle: a life-altering diagnosis of celiac disease that led to severe bone loss and osteoporosis.

  2. A different kind of battle

    The diagnosis led him to reflect on his own mortality, especially since his father passed away when he was just two months old. At his lowest point, he remembers telling his wife “I just want to dance with my daughter on her wedding day.” Determined to rewrite his life story, and be the father his didn’t get the chance to be, Kevin committed himself to not just improving his own health but also to helping others reclaim theirs.

  3. Becoming Bone Coach®

    Fueled by this sense of mission, Kevin founded BoneCoach.com and has helped hundreds of thousands of people with osteoporosis learn a natural approach to stronger bones as Bone Coach®.

  4. Healthy Bones Co.

    Yet, it wasn’t until years of working with clients, helping them develop a plan for stronger bones, that he realized an unmet need — truly effective, professional-grade supplements designed specifically for bone health. He knew that robust bones needed more than just exercise and dietary changes; they required targeted nutritional support.

  5. Nubonta

    Today, Healthy Bones Co. becomes Nubonta. The name, the look and the packaging are new; everything that made people trust Healthy Bones Co. stays the same. The same formulas and the same standards: research-supported ingredients in highly absorbable forms, made in the USA and third-party tested. Kevin’s mission hasn’t changed either: to help you build stronger bones for an active life.
