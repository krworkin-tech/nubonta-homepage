Healthy Bones Co. is now Nubonta

Is Nubonta the same as Healthy Bones Co.?

Yes. Nubonta is the new name of Healthy Bones Co. The formulas, the team and the standards stay the same; only the name and the packaging are new.

General

How do I take my supplements?

Daily dosages can be found on the supplement label and on our website for each product. If you’re looking for a plan for incorporating each of the foundational supplements into your daily routine, we’d encourage you to check out our Easy One-Day Guide. Always confirm what's best for you with your doctor.

Will your supplements be too large for me to swallow? I’m concerned they may be too big for me.

All of our supplement products have been designed to be as easy to take as possible. They are either narrow softgels or capsules that also deliver superior nutritional absorption to make a difference in your health.

Can I open your capsules and sprinkle the powder onto food?

Of course! You can definitely incorporate it into your diet. They are best mixed into applesauce or yogurt. Or add it to a Nubonta Collagen smoothie for a convenient and tasty way to boost your nutrient intake.

Can I cut your softgels open and add the contents to water?

Yes, if you find these softgels too big, you can safely cut them open with a scissors and add the contents to water or food before consuming.

Are your supplements suitable for both men and women? Are they safe for all ages?

Absolutely! Nubonta supplements are for both men and women of any age. Women just make up more of our client base since we’ve found there is greater awareness of bone loss and fracture risk and a desire to support and build bone strength.

With that being said, always confirm what's best for you with your doctor.

What makes Nubonta products different from all the others on the market?

Nubonta provides high-quality, professional-grade nutritional supplements for improved health and stronger bones support. They are backed by research, experience, and positive outcomes.

We know there are A LOT of supplement options out there, and most are not professional grade like ours.

Our supplements have...

  • Professional grade, high-quality ingredients
  • Good manufacturing processes (GMP certification)
  • Third party testing
  • Research-backed products
  • Proven efficacy
  • The most absorbable forms
  • No fillers or dyes
  • And thousands of happy customers

Our founder, Kevin Ellis, was diagnosed with osteoporosis at a young age, and his own health journey led to him creating Nubonta Now, it’s our team’s mission to provide high-quality, professional-grade products that truly make a difference for stronger bones support.

Are Nubonta products made in the USA?

Nubonta products are all manufactured in a GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice) registered facility in the USA from US and globally sourced ingredients.

What kind of third-party testing is done on your products? Are they tested for purity?

Nubonta products go through multiple layers of third-party testing to ensure potency, purity, and validation that every product meets the label claims.

Nubonta products are also manufactured in a GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice)-registered facility in the USA from US and globally sourced ingredients. Our GMP registration requires maintaining a strict set of compliance standards that ensure our products are safe, pure, and effective.

In addition, all third party labs used to test our products are International Organization for Standardization (ISO) certified.

Do you have a calcium supplement?

Yes! We have a supplement called OsteoElements Pro that contains calcium in addition to important nutrients like magnesium, vitamin D3, and vitamins K1 and K2 that are essential for the optimal absorption and utilization of calcium.

To fully optimize your bone health, we recommend pairing OsteoElements Pro with two of our best-selling bone support supplements:

  • Our Nubonta Collagen Peptides which includes FORTIBONE®, the only collagen peptide shown in clinical research to help improve bone mineral density and bone formation markers in postmenopausal women.
  • Our Annatrol™ Bone Support which contains Annatto Tocotrienols. Studies have shown 300mg of Annatto Tocotrienols decreased bone resorption (breakdown), and improved bone turnover rate in postmenopausal women.

Payment & discounts

What payment options do you have?

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, ShopPay, PayPal, and Google Pay.

Why isn’t my discount code working?

Our discount codes can only be used by each customer one time, and they can be applied to one-time purchases and first (new) “Subscribe & Save” orders. Subsequent Subscribe & Save orders will receive the standard 10% discount.

If your discount code isn’t working for your eligible order, please confirm that your spelling is correct and that you’re not including any spaces or punctuation when entering the code.

If you continue to encounter problems with your code, please reach out to our team at customercare@healthybonesco.com; we’re happy to help!

Subscriptions

How do I save 10% and sign up for a subscription?

Signing up for a subscription is a great idea!

You’ll not only save money, your subscription will make reordering effortless! Subscribing also makes sticking to a Nubonta plan easy. :)

On your next order do the following:

  1. Find the product you would like.
  2. Click on the "Subscribe & Save 10%" button (before clicking "add to cart").
  3. Choose how often you would like to receive the product: choose it from the drop-down.
  4. Click add to cart
  5. Review your order in the pop up window, and click checkout, or add another product.
  6. Your order will be shipped to you automatically after your purchase is completed.
Are there any additional fees that come with subscriptions?

There are no additional fees when you purchase a subscription. :)

Everyone receives 10% off on all subscriptionsregardless of how many different products are ordered. And if you live in the US, you’ll also receive free shipping on all orders over $129 (this includes all recurring subscription orders)!

Where do I go to modify my subscription?

Modifying your subscription is easy. Just follow the steps below:

  1. Navigate to your Nubonta account login page
  2. In the login window, enter the email address and password associated with your subscription account.
  3. Click the Sign inbutton.
  4. Once logged in, navigate to the ‘Manage subscriptions’ and make the changes you need.
What can I do if I’m not able to login to my account?

Not to worry! An account will be automatically created for you when you purchase a subscription. To continue setting up your account with a password, click “CREATE ACCOUNT” on the login page. On the next page, simply enter your first and last name, then type the same email address you used to place your subscription order, along with your preferred password. Once completed, you’ll be able to access your account and view your subscriptions. The system will recognize the password you’ve set every time you log in.

If you have already set up your account, simply click “Forgot your password?” on the login page and enter your email address on the next page. You will receive an email from Nubonta with the subject, “Customer Account Password Reset.” If the email is not in your inbox, kindly check your Spam or Junk folder.

Alternatively, you can click Manage Subscription on the login page and enter your email address on the next page. You will receive a direct link to your subscription portal, as well as the option to continue setting up your account with a password.

Can I pause my subscription?

Although our subscription system does not have a pause option, if you’re needing to adjust your subscription delivery date or frequency, our customer portal allows you to adjust your next order date, and/or skip a delivery if you need to.

You can do so by navigating to your Nubonta account login page and clicking ‘manage subscriptions’.

How to Adjust Your Next Order Date

Once logged in to your Nubonta account, you’ll have access to your Customer Subscription Portal.

Once you’re in the Customer Subscription Portal, follow the directions below to adjust your next order date:

  1. From your active subscriptions, choose the product subscription you’d like to adjust by clicking Manage this subscription.
  2. Click Reschedule.
  3. Choose the date you’d like to reschedule to.
  4. Click Save Changes.

How to Skip A Delivery

Log in to your Customer Subscription Portal through your Nubonta account.

Once you’re in the Customer Subscription Portal, follow the directions below to skip a delivery:

  1. From your active subscriptions, choose the product subscription you’d like to adjust by clicking Manage this subscription.
  2. Click Skip.
  3. Confirm that you’d like to skip your order by clicking Yes, skip from this order.

Shipping & orders

When will I receive my order?

Once you place your order, our system will begin to prepare it! Orders are shipped within 1-2 business days of placement.

After your order is shipped, please allow 3-5 business days for your order to arrive. As soon as your order is shipped, you'll receive an email with your tracking information.

Do you ship internationally?

At this time, Nubonta ships to the United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia, Austria, Belgium, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Ireland, Italy, the Netherlands, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Spain, Sweden, and Switzerland.

We’re currently in the process of expanding our services to several other countries.

Please refer to our Shipping Policy for details regarding certain countries we are unable to ship to due to their customs laws and restrictions. These countries include: Mexico, Afghanistan, Barbados, Belarus, Bhutan, Brunei, Cuba, Laos, Macao, Niger, Russia, Slovenia, South Sudan, Syria, Ukraine, Yemen, Brazil and South Africa.

Do you ship to PO Boxes?

Yes, Nubonta can ship to PO Boxes within the US.

What is shipping protection?

Shipping Protection is an optional delivery guarantee that protects your supplement order in the event your delivery is damaged, stolen, or lost during transit.

Shipping Protection charges a small fee at checkout. Use the slider button in the pop out window to turn the shipping protection on or off.

Will I be taxed on my shipment to Canada?

We do everything possible to use carriers that reduce the chance of incurring additional duties and taxes.

Please refer to our Shipping Policy for further details.

What company do you use to ship?

We work with trusted shipping carriers, including FedEx, DHL, and USPS. For orders within the U.S., the carrier is selected based on which option is the most efficient and reliable for your specific location.

This helps us ensure faster delivery times and a higher success rate for getting your order to you smoothly.

What is your return / refund policy?

Nubonta has a 30-day return policy, which means you have 30 days after receiving your item to request a return.

To be eligible for a return, your item must be in the same condition that you received it in, unopened, and in its original packaging.

Please refer to our Refund Policy for further details and next steps.
